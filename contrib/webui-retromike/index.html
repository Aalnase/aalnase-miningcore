MFLEX SOLO Pool FAQ

Current information for mining on test.go-poolmining.com

Do I need to register an account?

No. This pool uses your MFLEX wallet address as your account. Configure your miner with your payout address as the username and start mining. After your first valid shares, your miner appears automatically in the dashboard.

How do I connect my miner?

Use the Connect page for the current settings. For MFLEX the stratum endpoint is: 

stratum+tcp://test.go-poolmining.com:3333

Username format: 

YOUR_MFLEX_WALLET.worker1

Password can usually be x. If your miner supports static difficulty you can use d=DIFFICULTY, for example d=100000.

Which coin and algorithm does this pool mine?

The pool mines Multiflex / MFLEX with the Sha256D algorithm.

Is this PPLNS or SOLO?

This MFLEX pool runs in SOLO mode. It is not PPLNS. The miner that actually finds a valid block receives the block reward after the block is mature and the payout rules allow payment. Old PPLNS explanations do not apply to this pool.

Why can I submit shares but not receive a reward for every share?

In SOLO mining, shares prove that your miner is working, but only a share that solves a real network block creates a block reward. Normal shares help estimate hashrate and miner status; they are not paid one-by-one like PPS and they are not distributed across miners like PPLNS.

Where do I see my miner dashboard?

Open the Miners page and click your miner address. The WebUI opens the dashboard for that address. You can also open the Dashboard page directly and enter your MFLEX wallet address.

Why is my pool hashrate different from my local miner hashrate?

The pool estimates hashrate from submitted shares over time. It can take several minutes before the displayed value stabilizes. Short-term differences between your local miner and the pool dashboard are normal.

When will I get paid?

A payment can only happen after a valid block is found, accepted by the MFLEX network, and matured. Once the reward is mature and your balance reaches the configured minimum payout, Miningcore processes the payout automatically.

Why do new blocks show as pending?

Newly found blocks need network confirmations before they become mature. Until then they are shown as pending. This is normal blockchain behavior and protects the pool from paying out blocks that are not final yet.

Which time zone is shown in the Blocks table?

Miningcore stores and serves timestamps in UTC. The WebUI displays block and payment times converted to Europe/Berlin time for readability.

I need help. What should I send?

Send your MFLEX wallet address, miner software name/version, the stratum URL you use, and a short miner log excerpt showing connection or share errors. Never send private keys or wallet seed phrases.
